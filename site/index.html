Omac

Omarchy's window keys on your Mac.
One app. Nothing else to install.

A tiling window manager for macOS. Your windows arrange themselves side by side, never overlap, and you move between them with the keyboard.

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/evanscastonguay/omac/main/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later — nothing else. Then allow Omac under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
